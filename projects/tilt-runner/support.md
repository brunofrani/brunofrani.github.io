## Common questions

### How do I play?

Hold your iPhone or iPad and tilt it. The ball rolls the way you lean. Steer around the holes and pick up coins along the way. The run ends when the ball drops into a hole.

### A purchase is missing

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, not to your device. Check that you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy, then tap **Restore Purchases** in the game. If it still doesn't show up, email me with the date of the purchase and the order ID from Apple's receipt email.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all App Store payments, so refunds are requested from Apple rather than from me. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in, and choose **Request a refund**.

### Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your progress is saved on your device as you play.

### The game isn't working properly

Close the game completely and open it again, and check that you have the latest version from the App Store. If the problem continues, email me with the details listed above.

### Where should I play?

Tilt Runner is played by moving your device, so play somewhere safe and hold your device firmly. Never play while driving or walking.

### I have an idea for the game

I'd love to hear it. New balls, arenas, features, or anything that annoyed you: email me.
