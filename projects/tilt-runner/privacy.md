Tilt Runner is made by {{ site.author.name }}, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects and the choices you have.

## What the game collects

Tilt Runner has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or location.

- **Your progress** is saved on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. I can't access it.
- **Purchases** are processed by Apple, so I never see your payment details. [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) confirms and restores purchases using an anonymous ID and the details of the transaction.
- **Ads** are optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for a reward. They come from [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), which may collect information such as your IP address, your device type and, if you allow tracking, your advertising identifier.
- **Gameplay statistics**, such as runs played, scores and coins earned, are sent to [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice) with an anonymous ID, your device model and your approximate country, to help me improve the game.
- **Game Center**, if you're signed in, shows your best score and nickname on the leaderboard.
- **Support emails**: if you contact me, I receive your email address, your message, and any device details you choose to include.

Version 1.0 of the game only stores your progress on your device and uses RevenueCat for purchases. Ads, gameplay statistics, Game Center and iCloud arrive in version 2.

## Your choices

- **Tracking:** you can allow or deny tracking when the game asks, and change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland:** the game asks for your consent first. Ads are only personalized, and gameplay statistics only collected, if you agree. You can change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy Choices.

## How information is shared

I don't sell your personal information. It is shared only with the services named above, which may process it in other countries, or when the law requires it. Information is kept only for as long as it's needed.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, and to withdraw your consent. To make a request, email me at {% include contact-email.html %}. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Tilt Runner is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has shared information with me, contact me and I'll delete it.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Email me at {% include contact-email.html %}.
