This policy explains what information the Tilt Runner game for iPhone ("Tilt Runner" or "the game") collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. Tilt Runner is made by {{ site.author.name }}, an independent developer ("I", "me"), who is responsible for the information described here.

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
**The short version**

- Tilt Runner has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
- Your progress is stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. I can't see it.
- Video ads are optional and come from Google AdMob. Your advertising identifier is only shared if you allow it.
- Gameplay statistics help me improve the game. Where the law requires your consent, they are only collected if you give it.
- Payments are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details.
- I don't sell your personal information.

## Your progress, on your device and in iCloud

Tilt Runner saves your progress on your device: your high score, coins and energy, the worlds, balls, trails and arenas you've unlocked, and your settings.

The first time you play, the game creates a random player ID. It isn't based on your name, your device or your Apple Account. It exists only so that your progress, your purchases and the game's statistics all belong to the same player.

If iCloud is turned on, your progress and player ID are synced through iCloud so they carry over to your other devices and survive reinstalling the game. That copy is stored in your iCloud account under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and I have no access to it.

## Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to the Tilt Runner leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can sign out, or change who can see your profile, in iOS Settings → Game Center.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Tilt Runner shows video ads only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a reward, such as refilling your energy or doubling the coins from a run. The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

When an ad is requested, Google receives information such as your IP address, your device type, operating system and language, the app version, and how you interact with the ad. If you allow tracking (see below), Google also receives your device's advertising identifier and may use it to show ads based on your interests. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google explains this in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your choices

- **Tracking (everywhere).** The game asks whether it may track you, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose *Ask App Not to Track*, your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not targeted with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Consent (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland).** Before any ads or statistics are loaded, the game shows Google's consent form, where you can accept, decline, or choose individual purposes. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game, under Settings → Privacy Choices.

## Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

To find out how the game is actually played (whether the tutorial makes sense, where players get stuck, whether the coin economy is fair), Tilt Runner sends gameplay events to GameAnalytics, an analytics service built for games.

These events cover things like runs started and finished, scores, coins earned and spent, energy used, worlds unlocked, tutorial progress, ads watched and purchases made. They are sent together with your random player ID, your device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate country, which GameAnalytics works out from your IP address.

The statistics never include your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and I don't use them for advertising.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, statistics are only collected if you agree in the consent form. If you don't, the analytics service is never started and nothing is sent. See the [GameAnalytics privacy notice](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice).

## In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

You can buy coin packs in the game. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. I never receive your name, your Apple Account details or your payment information.

To confirm a purchase, add the coins to your balance and let you restore purchases later, Tilt Runner uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your random player ID, the details of each App Store transaction (which product, its price and currency, the date, and Apple's transaction identifiers), your app and operating system versions, and your IP address. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Crash reports

If you've turned on *Share With App Developers* in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple sends me crash reports and performance data for the game. They are not linked to you, and I use them only to fix problems.

## When you contact me

If you email me, including with *Send Feedback* in the game's settings, I receive your email address and whatever you write. *Send Feedback* also adds some details to the message that make bug reports easier to act on: the app version, iOS version, device model, your high score, your coin balance, and the ball and arena you've selected. You can read and edit them before you send. I use your message only to reply and to fix the problem you've reported.

## How the information is used

- To run the game and keep your progress.
- To process, deliver and restore purchases.
- To show the ads you choose to watch, and to give you their rewards.
- To understand how the game is played and make it better.
- To answer your messages and fix bugs.
- To prevent fraud and abuse.

I don't sell your personal information. If you allow tracking, Google can use your advertising identifier to personalize ads, which some privacy laws (California's, for example) treat as "sharing" personal information. You can stop this at any time by turning off tracking as described above.

## Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, I rely on:

- **Performance of a contract**, to save your progress and to process and restore your purchases.
- **Your consent**, for personalized ads and for gameplay statistics. You can withdraw it at any time, as described above.
- **Legitimate interests**, to show non-personalized ads that keep the game free, to answer your messages, to fix crashes, and to keep the game secure and free of fraud.

## Who receives information

Information is shared only with the services described above, and only for the purposes described above:

- **Apple**: iCloud, Game Center, App Store payments and crash reports.
- **Google**: ads, through AdMob.
- **GameAnalytics**: gameplay statistics.
- **RevenueCat**: purchase validation and restoring purchases.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of players or myself. If Tilt Runner is ever transferred to someone else, its information may be transferred with it, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

## International transfers

These services may process information in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long information is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete the game.
- **In iCloud:** until you remove it from your iCloud account.
- **With GameAnalytics and RevenueCat:** for as long as they need it to provide their service to me, under their own retention policies.
- **Emails:** only for as long as I need them to help you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information held about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw your consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email me at {% include contact-email.html %}. Because Tilt Runner has no accounts, I may need your help to find your information, for example the order ID of a purchase, which you can find in your Apple Account's purchase history. I will reply within 30 days.

## Children

Tilt Runner is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age for consenting to data processing where you live, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given me personal information, please contact me and I will delete it.

## Security

The game and the services it uses send information over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable care to protect the information described here.
